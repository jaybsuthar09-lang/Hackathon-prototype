The problem

Citizens currently access different municipal services through multiple portals and applications.

  • Multiple user accounts
  • Fragmented service delivery
  • Poor citizen experience
  • Limited service visibility

There is a need for a unified platform providing a single point of access for complaints, payments, certificates, permits, public information, and service tracking.
